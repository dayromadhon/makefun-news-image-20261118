Lead · Diplomacy

XI AND PUTIN MEET IN SHENZHEN

Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin began talks in Shenzhen on Wednesday at the APEC summit, a day before a first-ever three-way meeting with President Donald Trump. No agenda for the trilateral has been published. Trump says he wants nuclear arms control central; Beijing resists making it the centrepiece.

The trilateral with President Trump is set for Thursday.
Middle East · Gulf

US STRIKES IRAN; IRAN HITS BACK IN THE GULF

US officials say jets struck a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz. Iran says its drones then hit US sites in Kuwait and Bahrain; Bahrain says it intercepted several attacks before dawn.

Europe · Conflict

KERCH BRIDGE ROAD SPAN FALLS INTO THE SEA

After a second day of strikes, the road arch of the Kerch Bridge to Crimea fell into the strait, Ukraine’s General Staff says; video verified by Reuters shows the span down. Rail traffic partly resumed. Crimea’s Russian-installed governor says two people were killed.

United States · Politics

PELTOLA WINS ALASKA; DEMOCRATS TAKE 56–44 SENATE

Democrat Mary Peltola defeats Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan, the AP projects, settling the last Senate race of the midterms. Democrats take a 56–44 majority, their largest since 2009. Peltola is the first Alaska Native elected to the Senate.

Science · Space

VOYAGER 1, ONE LIGHT-DAY FROM EARTH

NASA’s Voyager 1 crossed one light-day from Earth at 5:16 a.m. ET — about 16 billion miles — the first human-made object to reach the distance. Its radio signals now take a full day to arrive.
