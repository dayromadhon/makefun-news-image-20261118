  making it the centrepiece.
- **Middle East · Gulf:** US strikes Iran; Iran hits back. US officials say jets
  struck a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of
  Hormuz. Iran says its drones then hit US sites in Kuwait and Bahrain; Bahrain
  says it intercepted several attacks before dawn.
- **Europe · Conflict:** Kerch Bridge road span falls. After a second day of
  strikes, the road arch of the Kerch Bridge to Crimea fell into the strait,
  Ukraine's General Staff says; video verified by Reuters shows the span down.
  Rail traffic partly resumed. Crimea's Russian-installed governor says two
  people were killed.
- **United States · Politics:** Peltola wins Alaska. Democrat Mary Peltola
  defeats Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan, the AP projects, settling the last
  Senate race of the midterms. Democrats take a 56–44 majority, their largest
  since 2009. Peltola is the first Alaska Native elected to the Senate.
- **Science · Space:** Voyager 1, one light-day from Earth. NASA's Voyager 1
  crossed one light-day from Earth at 5:16 a.m. ET — about 16 billion miles —
  the first human-made object to reach the distance. Its radio signals now take
  a full day to arrive.
- **Also today:** DR Congo Ebola outbreak passes 11,000 cases, 5,400 deaths —
  second-largest on record, WHO says; US loses measles-elimination status —
  5,412 cases in 2026, the most since 1991, PAHO says; Nvidia posts a record
  $115.8B quarter and its board says Huang will not return as CEO.

All stories were verified against reporting published on 18 November 2026.
Disputed claims are attributed to the side making them; no unpublished summit
agenda is stated.
